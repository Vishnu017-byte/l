Privacy policy

This site collects only the information needed to operate the lost-and-found service, such as account details, submitted reports, and claim activity.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address, password hash, and optional phone number for account creation.
  • Lost/found item details, location, dates, and any uploaded item photos.
  • Notifications, claims, and administrative activity relevant to resolving reports.

How we use it

  • To operate user accounts and secure authentication.
  • To match and route lost/found reports between users.
  • To notify users about item matches, claims, and status updates.
  • To support site administration and moderation.

Data protection

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect information stored in the platform. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Content is retained for operational use as long as required by the site’s functionality and institutional policy. Users may request account or report updates through the site administrator.

This privacy policy may be revised as the service evolves.
